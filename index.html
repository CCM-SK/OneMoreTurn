Tap a glowing tile, or use the arrow keys, to move.

How to play
  • Every turn you move one tile, up/down/left/right, onto a lit neighbor.
  • Unmarked tiles are hidden. Stepping on one reveals it: blue is +1, gold is +5, and empty ground is safe but scoreless.
  • Red ground gives way — you lose every point from the current run.
  • Every 5 moves the mine shifts: unrevealed tiles reshuffle, and the odds get worse. The "Unstable" bar shows what share of hidden tiles are dangerous right now.
  • Bank at any time to lock in your run's points. There's no penalty for stopping — only for pushing on.
